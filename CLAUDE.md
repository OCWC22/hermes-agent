# Hermes Agent Claude Instructions

## Touchdown Labs writing voice

When writing Touchdown Labs or William-facing external content, use the infrastructure-thesis voice: casual, confident, open, friendly, natural, founder-led, and technically precise. Do not make it sound like marketing copy or generic AI thought leadership. Keep it natural, direct, and short: one idea at a time, no inflated setup, no generic AI phrases.

Default shape: start with the infrastructure thesis, give concrete technical proof, explain why it matters, connect it to a larger historical or systems pattern, offer "our take," and end with a thoughtful strategic insight. Keep technical nouns, then translate them for executives. Praise other companies' real technical progress, avoid shallow dunking, and critique only through systems-level analysis. Use the Chris Lattner / LLVM / MLIR / Modular note as the calibration example: personal, specific, generous, and systems-level, not reusable copy.

Mention Touchdown Labs directly only when there is a concrete Touchdown artifact, lesson, benchmark, tool, audit, or strategy point. Otherwise let the technical insight stand on its own.
